Climate Risk

for California Properties

How to Use

  1. Navigate to an individual property listing on a supported real estate website.
  2. Click the Climate Risk extension icon to open the side panel.
  3. Climate risk data will load automatically for the property.

About

This extension displays climate risk information for California properties, including:

  • Wildfire risk
  • Flood zones
  • Sea level rise
  • Extreme heat
  • Extreme heat days
  • Extreme precipitation

Data Sources

  • CAL FIRE
  • FEMA
  • CA Coastal Commission
  • Cal-Adapt

Privacy

This extension processes property addresses and coordinates solely to display climate risk data. No personal data is collected, shared, or transmitted to third parties beyond the public APIs listed above. Cached data is stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time via Chrome settings.

Disclaimer: This information is for educational purposes only. Consult with professionals before making real estate decisions.
