Climate Risk

for California Properties

How to Use

  1. Navigate to an individual property listing on a supported real estate website.
  2. A badge dot (●) appears on the extension icon when you're on a property page.
  3. Click the Climate Risk icon to open the side panel.
  4. Climate risk data loads automatically and updates as you browse listings.

Supported sites: Zillow · Redfin · Realtor.com · Trulia · Compass · Homes.com

About

This extension displays free climate risk information for California properties, including:

  • 🔥 Wildfire risk (CAL FIRE zones)
  • 🌊 Flood zones (FEMA NFHL)
  • ☀️ Extreme heat (mid-century projections)
  • 🔆 Extreme heat days (days above 100°F)
  • 🌧️ Extreme precipitation
  • 📈 Sea level rise (coastal properties)

Data is read from the browser URL bar — no page modification, no scraping.

Data Sources

  • CAL FIRE
  • FEMA
  • CA Coastal Commission
  • Cal-Adapt

Privacy

This extension processes property addresses and coordinates solely to display climate risk data. No personal data is collected, shared, or transmitted to third parties beyond the public APIs listed above. Cached data is stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time via Chrome settings.
