About & Data Sources

This extension displays climate risk information for California properties, including:

  • Wildfire risk
  • Flood zones
  • Sea level rise
  • Extreme heat
  • Extreme heat days
  • Extreme precipitation

Data Sources

  • CAL FIRE
  • FEMA
  • CA Coastal Commission
  • Cal-Adapt

Privacy

Property addresses and coordinates are processed locally to display climate risk data. No personal data is collected, shared, or transmitted beyond the public APIs listed above. Cached data is stored on your device only.
